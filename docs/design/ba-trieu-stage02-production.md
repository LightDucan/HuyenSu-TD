# Bà Triệu Stage 02 — Công Phá Thành Ấp

## Identity and historical boundary

- Chapter: `chapter-ba-trieu-248`
- Stage: `bt-02-cong-pha-thanh-ap`
- Map: `map-bt-thanh-ap`
- Theme: `cuu-chan-settlement`
- Period: 248 CE
- Faction: Đông Ngô
- Allowed Heroes: `ba-trieu`, `trieu-quoc-dat`, `dinh-boi`
- Historical confidence: **GENERAL CITY ATTACK SUPPORTED; SPECIFIC TƯ PHỐ BATTLE RECONSTRUCTED**

Tam Quốc Chí supports the general event that rebels attacked administrative settlements in Giao Chỉ and Cửu Chân in 248. The exact settlement, walls, gates, troop numbers and sequence represented here are gameplay reconstruction. Player-facing text therefore uses “một thành ấp / đồn trị Đông Ngô tại Cửu Chân” and does not present Tư Phố as a confirmed battle site.

## Stage role

After Stage 01 teaches the Bà Triệu roster and mixed Wu formations, Stage 02 concentrates pressure into settlement lanes: a breach choke, a long courtyard lane, inner turns and sustained armored/ranged counterattacks. The existing tower-defense rules remain unchanged; the offensive event is represented as holding a forward foothold while the garrison counterattacks.

Target first-clear duration is approximately 13–19 minutes at x1. x3 and Auto Wave shorten the practical session.

The stage has exactly 20 unique IDs, `bt-02-wave-01` through `bt-02-wave-20`, totaling 268 enemies. `wu-field-commander` appears only on Wave 20 and is a generic gameplay composite, never a named historical officer or Lục Dận.

## Dedicated map

`map-bt-thanh-ap` is a 1024×768, 12×10 map with one bounded, non-intersecting ten-segment route: outside approach → breach → inner lane → courtyard → exit. Ten unique placement tiles provide breach-control, long-lane, inner-courtyard, flexible and situational positions. Settlement, earth, barrier, forest, rock and camp decorations are visual-only and provide no movement, attack, blocking, elevation or pathfinding effects.

## Narrative and outcome

The generic narrative contract provides a concise reconstruction pre-battle card and beats at Waves 1, 7, 14 and 20. Victory means the local garrison resistance is broken and this settlement position is secured; it does not claim that Đông Ngô or all Cửu Chân is defeated. Defeat is tactical: the breachhead collapses and the assault force regroups.

## Scope boundary

Stage 01 remains the locked 18-wave Núi Nưa production stage. Stages 03–06 remain their existing three-wave prototypes. No new mode, siege mechanic, economy rebalance, save migration, combat math or fake art is included.
