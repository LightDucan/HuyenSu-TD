# HBT Stage 01 enemy walk brief

- `han-sword-infantry`: early Eastern Han infantry silhouette, moderate protection and simple weapon.
- `han-crossbow-soldier`: clearly readable crossbow silhouette, lighter than armored units.
- `han-armored-guard`: heavier, slower silhouette; avoid late Ming/Qing plate language.
- `boss-ma-vien`: distinct early Eastern Han commander silhouette, no fantasy weapon or Three Kingdoms styling.

Each approved walk sheet, when supplied, is `src/assets/enemies/<enemy-id>/walk.png`, transparent RGBA, an 8-frame horizontal 1024×128 strip with 128×128 frames, right-facing and stable feet baseline. No binary sheets are currently bundled; primitive fallback remains required.
