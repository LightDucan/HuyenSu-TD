# Bà Triệu Stage 02 — Player Progression

## Unlock chain

The existing campaign chain is preserved:

1. Complete HBT Stage 01 → Bà Triệu Stage 01 becomes available.
2. Complete `bt-01-tu-nghia-nui-nua` → `bt-02-cong-pha-thanh-ap` becomes available.
3. Complete `bt-02-cong-pha-thanh-ap` → Stage 03 becomes available.

The Chapter remains `chapter-ba-trieu-248`; no IDs are renamed and Stages 03–06 are not productionized by this task.

## Roster and rewards

Stage 02 accepts only the existing Bà Triệu production roster: Bà Triệu, Triệu Quốc Đạt and Đinh Bôi. Missing Hero artwork uses the shared safe fallback and never blocks battle entry.

Ordinary clear reward remains unchanged:

- Gold: 22
- KNB: 1
- Anh Hồn: 10

There is no new first-clear package. Enemy rewards continue to use Balance V1 and existing idempotency/persistence semantics.

## Player-facing intent

No full tutorial chain is repeated. The stage communicates its tactical demand through the breach/settlement layout, mixed formations and short contextual narrative beats. Placement, x1/x3, Auto Wave and equipment-lock rules are inherited unchanged.
