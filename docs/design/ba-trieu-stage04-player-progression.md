# Bà Triệu — Stage 04 player progression

The chapter path remains ordered:

`Hai Bà Trưng completion → BT01 → BT02 → BT03 → BT04 → BT05 prototype`

`bt-04-lap-luy-bo-dien` is locked until `bt-03-ben-song-ma` is completed. After BT03 completion it becomes available; completing BT04 unlocks BT05. A completed BT04 remains replayable. This uses the shared campaign progression selector and does not add stage-specific UI or combat rules.

Stage 04 uses the existing Bà Triệu roster: Bà Triệu, Triệu Quốc Đạt and Đinh Bôi. It repeats no tutorial flow. Rewards remain 26 Gold, 1 KNB and 12 Anh Hồn, with no first-clear bonus. Stage 05–06 are intentionally not productionized.
