## Source layering and ending boundary

T1 records that the Giao Châu uprising was suppressed in 248 and that Lục Dận directed the suppression; Lục Dận survived. T2 records Triệu Ẩu/Bà Triệu tử trận. The association of the final stand with Núi Tùng and the account of tuẫn tiết belong to T3/local tradition. The stage text keeps these layers distinct and never claims that Tam Quốc Chí records a Battle of Núi Tùng.

Completing the stage is a mechanical completion of the Last Stand, not a military victory over Đông Ngô. No death mechanic, graphic scene, Lục Dận boss or Lục Dận casualty is implemented; the dignified historical ending is narrative/result text only.

## Waves, map and rewards

There are exactly 28 waves and 653 enemies. The generic `wu-field-commander` appears only in Wave 28. Stage-specific timing uses 600/1000/1550/2150ms offsets with 750ms sword, 875ms crossbow, 1075ms armored and 1450ms commander intervals.

The dedicated map is 1024×768 on a 12×10 grid with one bounded, axis-aligned, non-intersecting route and ten placements. The locked ordinary clear reward is **35 Gold / 2 KNB / 20 Anh Hồn** with no first-clear package.
