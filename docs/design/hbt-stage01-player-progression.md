# Stage 01 player progression

Before entry the player has the HBT starter roster, starter wallet/inventory and Command Energy governed by the existing meta runtime. A first clear targets a provisional onboarding bonus of 100 Gold, 50 KNB and 100 Anh Hồn, in addition to ordinary combat/stage rewards. This enables several Gold pulls and an initial KNB/recruitment/evolution-economy sample without bypassing Level 100 requirements. Random gacha outcomes are not guaranteed. Replay grants only ordinary repeatable rewards.
