# Bà Triệu Stage 01 — Player Progression

## Entry gate

The production chapter remains `chapter-ba-trieu-248`. It unlocks only after completion of `hbt-lang-bac-stage-01`. Stage 01 accepts the locked roster IDs:

- `ba-trieu`
- `trieu-quoc-dat`
- `dinh-boi`

Missing production Hero art does not block entry; portrait and battle rendering use the safe fallback boundary.

## Clear and replay

Completing `bt-01-tu-nghia-nui-nua` records campaign completion through the existing Meta V6 campaign runtime and unlocks the current Stage 02 progression path. A replay remains available after completion.

Ordinary clear reward remains unchanged:

- Gold: 20
- KNB: 1
- Anh Hồn: 10

Enemy kill rewards continue to use Balance V1. Stage 01 does not define a separate large first-clear package; this is explicitly ordinary-only for C08. Existing reward idempotency and campaign completion semantics are unchanged.

## Onboarding boundary

The full HBT introductory hint chain is not repeated. Stage 01 relies on the already-learned placement, x1/x3, Auto Wave and wave equipment-lock rules. Its tactical teaching comes from mixed enemy formations, differentiated placement positions and the four lightweight narrative beats.

## Locked non-goals

No Stage 02–06 production content, recruitment redesign, Hero evolution, new currency, balance overhaul, save migration or new combat mechanic is included.
