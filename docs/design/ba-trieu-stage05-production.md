# Bà Triệu — Stage 05: Đại Chiến Bồ Điền

## Production identity

- Stage ID: `bt-05-dai-chien-bo-dien`
- Chapter: `chapter-ba-trieu-248`
- Map: `map-bt-bo-dien-battle` (`bo-dien-field`)
- Period/faction: 248 CE / Đông Ngô
- Allowed Heroes: Bà Triệu, Triệu Quốc Đạt, Đinh Bôi
- Historical confidence: **COMPOSITE RECONSTRUCTION**

Stage 05 is a broad open-field pressure scenario around the Bồ Điền position. It uses the existing fixed-path, single-target tower-defense runtime; terrain decorations are presentation-only and add no combat mechanics.

## Historical boundary

Sources support that Lục Dận directed the Đông Ngô suppression campaign in Giao Châu in 248. The exact major battle shown here is a composite gameplay reconstruction informed by later/local tradition. The stage does not claim Lục Dận personally fought on this field, does not make him a boss, and does not depict his death or a permanent Đông Ngô defeat. No 8,000-troop claim is used.

## Waves and timing

There are exactly 26 waves and 539 enemies. The generic `wu-field-commander` appears only in Wave 26. Stage-specific spawn groups use 600/1050/1650/2250ms offsets with 775ms sword, 900ms crossbow, 1125ms armored and 1500ms commander intervals.

## Map and presentation

The map is 1024×768 on a 12×10 grid with one bounded, axis-aligned, non-intersecting fixed route and ten unique placement tiles. Earth, forest, rock, camp and barrier decorations are visual only.

## Rewards and progression

The locked ordinary clear reward is **30 Gold / 2 KNB / 15 Anh Hồn**, with no first-clear package. Completing BT04 unlocks Stage 05; completing Stage 05 unlocks the existing three-wave Stage 06 prototype.
